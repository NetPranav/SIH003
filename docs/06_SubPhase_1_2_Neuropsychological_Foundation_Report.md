## 2. Ribot’s Law Application Framework

### 2.1 Neuroanatomical Mechanism: Hippocampus vs. Neocortex
In Alzheimer's Disease (AD) and Vascular Dementia (VaD), the earliest neuropathological lesions (neurofibrillary tangles and amyloid plaques) aggregate predominantly within the **entorhinal cortex and hippocampal CA1 subfield**. This anatomical destruction impairs the consolidation of new short-term memories (anterograde amnesia).

Conversely, autobiographical memories acquired during adolescence and early adulthood (ages 10–30, clinically known as the **"Reminiscence Bump"**) have undergone complete systems consolidation, widely distributed across neocortical temporal-parietal networks.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   HIPPOCAMPAL CA1                      │
│        [Severe Synaptic Loss & Amyloid Deposition]     │
│        ✖ Recent Memory: "What did you eat at 8 AM?"    │
│        ✖ Short-Term Working Memory: Fails / Agitates   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                           ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             NEOCORTICAL TEMPORAL NETWORKS              │
│       [Preserved Long-Term Episodic & Praxic Engrams]  │
│       ✔ Ribot's Law: Remote Memories are Resilient     │
│       ✔ Stimuli: Pepa / Dhol, Muga Looms, Tea Gardens   │
│       ✔ Outcome: Emotional Reassurance & Neural Re-use │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.2 Culturally Grounded Sensory Anchors (NER Specific)

* **Gastronomic & Executive Planning**: Authentic traditional ingredients (*Masor Tenga*, *Khar*, *Bamboo Shoot*).

## 4. Digital MMSE / MoCA Multi-Domain Proxy Model

To provide longitudinal clinical utility to visiting ASHA workers, PHC medical officers, and family caregivers, Smriti-NER mathematically projects daily in-game telemetry into a continuous **Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) Proxy Score** ($\widehat{MMSE} \in [0, 30]$).

### 4.1 Mathematical Formulation
$$\widehat{MMSE} = \sum_{k=1}^{5} w_k \cdot S_k$$
where each domain score $S_k$ is computed via a calibrated logistic transfer function:
$$S_k = \text{MaxPoints}_k \cdot \left( \frac{1}{1 + \exp\left(-\lambda (x_k - \mu_k)\right)} \right)$$
* $\lambda = 5.2$ represents the clinical inflection slope calibrated against LASI Wave-1 cognitive cohorts.
* $x_k \in [0, 1]$ is the normalized telemetry vector for domain $k$.

### 4.2 Domain Weighting Matrix

| Domain | Weight ($w_k$) | Max Points | In-Game Telemetry Origin | Clinical Significance |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
| **Orientation ($S_{orient}$)** | $0.167$ | 5.0 | Date awareness, seasonal Haat ingredient choice, time-of-day greetings | Temporal & environmental orientation |
| **Working Memory ($S_{memory}$)** | $0.267$ | 8.0 | *Dhol-Pepa* auditory sequence length & *Weaver's Loom* span | Immediate & delayed auditory-motor recall |
| **Attention & Visual Search ($S_{attention}$)** | $0.233$ | 7.0 | *Kaziranga Safari* visual search time ($1/RT_{delib}$) & distractor rejection | Selective & sustained visual attention |
| **Executive Function ($S_{executive}$)** | $0.200$ | 6.0 | *Daily Haat* multi-ingredient recipe planning & basket sorting | Planning, categorization, and mental flexibility |
| **Language & Cultural Recognition ($S_{language}$)** | $0.133$ | 4.0 | Native vocabulary identification (*অসমীয়া*, *ꯃꯩꯇꯩ*, etc.) | Semantic memory & naming ability |
| **Total** | **1.000** | **30.0** | **Comprehensive Longitudinal Assessment** | **Standardized Clinical Staging** |

### 4.3 Standardized Clinical Staging Scale
* **$\widehat{MMSE} \ge 25.0$**: **Cognitively Intact** (Age-appropriate baseline; preventive cognitive wellness).
* **$20.0 \le \widehat{MMSE} \le 24.9$**: **Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI)** (Target for proactive daily Reminiscence Therapy).
* **$13.0 \le \widehat{MMSE} \le 19.9$**: **Moderate Dementia** (Requires intensive caregiver co-play & ASHA home visits).
* **$\widehat{MMSE} < 13.0$**: **Severe Decline** (Primary alert dispatched to district healthcare center).

## 5. Compassionate Interaction Design (CID) & Anti-Agitation Circuit Breaker (AACB)

### 5.1 The "Zero Negative Reinforcement" Doctrine
Standard educational software employs negative reinforcement (buzzers, red cross marks, "Game Over" screens, time penalties). In dementia care, these stimuli induce **acute catastrophic reactions**—profound panic, weeping, aggressive agitation, and permanent device rejection.

Smriti-NER enforces strict **Compassionate Interaction Design (CID)**:
1. **Zero Failure Audio**: No harsh descending tones or error buzzers exist in the audio codebase. Incorrect touches produce soft, calming woodblock or river notes.
2. **Zero Defeat Visuals**: No red crosses, countdown bars, or strike counters.
3. **No Time Constraints**: Timers run silently in the background for clinical telemetry analysis; the patient is never presented with an anxious ticking clock.

### 5.2 Anti-Agitation Circuit Breaker (AACB) Formulation
The engine computes the real-time **Agitation Vulnerability Index ($AVI_t$)**:
$$AVI_t = \sum_{j=0}^{k-1} 0.85^j \cdot \mathbb{I}(Y_{t-j} = 0) + 0.40 \cdot \max\left(0, \frac{RT_{delib} - \mu_{RT}}{\sigma_{RT}}\right)$$

When $AVI_t \ge 1.70$ (e.g., 2 consecutive errors or 1 error combined with extreme hesitation $Z_{RT} > 2.0$), the AACB triggers instant deterministic attenuation:
* **Golden Breathing Halo**: The correct answer is highlighted in an animated golden glow (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.45)`).
* **Visual Clutter Reduction**: All distractor options are smoothly dimmed to 40% opacity.
* **Hitbox Expansion**: Touch target boundaries expand by +25% to tolerate senile motor tremor.
* **Familiar Audio Cueing**: The system voice prompt switches to the pre-recorded voice of the elder's grandchild: *"Take your time, Bor-Deuta. Let's look together at the golden glowing instrument..."*

## 6. Bi-Factor Latency Decomposition (Tremor Isolation)

To eliminate the clinical bias of penalizing physical arthritic or essential tremors as cognitive delay, the engine decomposes raw interaction latency:

$$\tau_{motor} = \kappa \cdot \ln(\Omega_{wander}) + \delta_{baseline}$$
$$RT_{delib} = RT_{total} - \tau_{motor}$$

Where:
* $\Omega_{wander} = \frac{S_{path}}{\max(S_{disp}, 1.0)}$ measures coordinate jitter along the touch trajectory.
* Confident direct taps yield $\Omega_{wander} \approx 1.0 \implies \tau_{motor} \approx \delta_{baseline} \approx 280\text{ ms}$.
* Senile tremors yield $\Omega_{wander} > 1.8 \implies \tau_{motor} \approx 420\text{ ms}$ isolated, preventing cognitive difficulty inflation.

## 7. Circadian Sundowning Anomaly Detection

**Sundowning Syndrome** manifests as agitation, confusion, and wandering occurring in dementia patients as natural daylight fades (4:00 PM – 7:30 PM).

The **Circadian Anomaly Index ($CAI_d$)** monitors divergence:
$$CAI_d = \frac{\overline{RT}_{delib}(d, \mathcal{W}_{sundown}) - \overline{RT}_{delib}(d, \mathcal{W}_{morning})}{\sigma_{RT}(d)} + \frac{\text{Mistakes}(d, \mathcal{W}_{sundown})}{\max(1, \text{Mistakes}(d, \mathcal{W}_{morning}))}$$

### Clinical Intervention Protocol:
* When $CAI_d > 2.35$ on two consecutive days:
  1. Caregiver dashboard logs an alert: *"Mild evening disorientation pattern detected between 5 PM and 6:30 PM."*
  2. Automatic reminder: *"Ensure room is brightly lit before dusk, close window curtains to avoid confusing shadows, and engage calming Bihu flute tracks."*
  3. Pre-sundowning Reminiscence Therapy (Session C) is automatically prioritized at 4:30 PM.

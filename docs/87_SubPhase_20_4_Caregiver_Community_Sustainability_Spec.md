## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Intent

While Sub-Phases 20.1–20.3 established the statutory data governance policies, institutional 5-year budget lines (₹38.40 Cr), and software engineering continuous improvement pipelines, **Sub-Phase 20.4 delivers the ultimate human and community infrastructure**: transforming Smriti-NER from an external technological intervention into an indigenous, self-sustaining, and community-owned public health movement across Northeast India.

Sub-Phase 20.4 tackles two critical post-launch imperatives:
1. **Ongoing Peer-Support Network Maintenance**: Sustaining the 16 district caregiver co-operatives and psycho-social safety nets so that family caregivers do not suffer isolation or burnout once research grant supervision transitions to district health teams.
2. **Community Circle Franchise Model**: Providing a turnkey, open-access, zero-royalty operational playbook enabling any Primary Health Centre (PHC), Anganwadi, local NGO (e.g., ARDSI, HelpAge India), church/village council, or tea garden welfare committee to launch, facilitate, and govern autonomous Reminiscence Circles without software developer assistance.
3. **Milestone M20 Formal Certification**: Establishing the statutory closure of the Smriti-NER Master Roadmap, validating all 5 institutional sustainability gates (≥50,000 patient trajectory, ₹38.40 Cr multi-year funding, autonomous CAB governance, 32 self-running community circles, and open-source core licensing).

## 2. Component 1: Ongoing Caregiver Peer-Support Maintenance Plan

Caring for an elder with Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) or early-to-moderate dementia carries a severe neuro-psychiatric burden. Sub-Phase 20.4 institutes a permanent, decentralized Caregiver Support Maintenance Architecture.

### 2.1 ZBI-12 Stratified Caregiver Peer Matching

Caregivers are autonomously clustered into micro-affinity groups (5–7 caregivers per circle) using local-first matching criteria:
- **Zarit Burden Interview (ZBI-12) Tier**: Mild (<10), Moderate (10–20), Severe (>20).
- **Clinical Staging Concordance**: FAST Stage 3–4 (MCI/Early) vs. Stage 5–6 (Moderate/Sundowning).
- **Dialect & Cultural Background**: Matching Assamese, Bodo, Bengali, Khasi, Garo, Mizo, Meitei, and Nepali speakers.
- **Geographic Proximity**: Micro-neighborhood clustering within 5 km to enable offline respite swapping.

### 2.2 Four Regional Caregiver Co-operative Hubs

To ensure decentralized regional oversight across all 8 NER states, four administrative and clinical Co-operative Hubs operate under state healthcare directorates:

| Regional Hub | Jurisdictions Covered | Base Institution | Regional Coordinator | Key Support Services |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Brahmaputra Valley Hub** | Assam (10 Districts) | GMCH Guwahati | Dr. Bhupen Hazarika Center | Assamese/Bodo circles, Tea garden mobile van, respite exchange |
| **Eastern Hills Hub** | Manipur, Nagaland | RIMS Imphal | Kangleipak Geriatric Cell | Meitei/Nagamese circles, conflict-sensitive family support, IVR audio circles |
| **Southern Highland Hub** | Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura | NEIGRIHMS Shillong | Khasi Hills Community Trust | Khasi/Garo/Mizo circles, church-partnered Sunday elder groups, caregiver wellness retreats |
| **Himalayan Northern Hub** | Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh | SMIMS Gangtok / TRIHMS Naharlagun | Kanchenjunga Elder Forum | Nepali/Nyishi/Monpa circles, high-altitude remote IVR check-in, seasonal isolation mitigation |

### 2.3 Respite Care Voucher Program

Caregiver burnout is mitigated through the **Smriti Monthly Respite Voucher**:
- **Allocation**: 16 hours of subsidized in-home companion care per family per month for caregivers with ZBI-12 ≥ 16.
- **Funding Stream**: Co-financed via NPHCE District Disability & Geriatric Welfare Funds (₹1,800/family/month).
- **Trained Respite Cadre**: Certified Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANMs) and senior ASHA Saheli volunteers trained in basic cognitive stimulation and safe elder engagement.
- **Redemption & Verification**: Verified via ABHA ID QR code scan with offline PIN authorization.

### 2.4 Bi-Weekly Multilingual Psychoeducation & Tele-MANAS Escalation

- **Bi-Weekly Interactive Webinars & IVR Audio Conferences**: Broadcast simultaneously across all 8 regional languages covering non-pharmacological behavioral management (sundowning de-escalation, nutrition, hydration, sleep hygiene).
- **Direct Tele-MANAS (14416) Integration**: One-touch national mental health helpline routing directly from the Caregiver PWA and IVR portal with pre-briefed geriatric psychiatry dispatch in Guwahati, Shillong, and Imphal.

### 3.1 Turnkey Facilitator Toolkit

Each certified franchise receives a standardized physical and digital implementation bundle:

1. **Facilitator Field Handbook (`SMRITI-CIR-HBK-2026`)**:
   - Published in 8 regional languages.
   - Step-by-step 60-minute session templates: (1) Arrival & herbal tea welcoming (10 min), (2) Sensory trigger exploration (15 min), (3) Group storytelling & folk songs (20 min), (4) Light cooperative memory game / bamboo rhythm tap (10 min), (5) Closing gratitude & family reunion (5 min).
2. **Tactile Cultural Memory Trigger Kit**:
   - Standardized physical sensory objects: Assamese Eri silk swatch, brass Bell-metal cup (Kahi/Bati), Khasi pinecone & betel pouch (Kwai), Mizo Puan textile strip, Himalayan cardamom & cinnamon aroma vials, and vintage brass bell.
3. **PWA Reminiscence Display Pack**:
   - Pre-cached offline photo slides, vintage audio clips (Bhupen Hazarika classics, Bihu beats, Khasi choral hymns, Manipuri Nata Sankirtana).
4. **Safeguarding & Informed Consent Forms**:
   - Pictographic, vernacular consent waivers respecting village tribal authority and DPDP Act 2023 norms.

### 3.2 4-Module "Train-the-Facilitator" Certification

Aspiring circle leaders (NGO workers, retired schoolteachers, youth volunteers, ASHA workers) complete a 12-hour micro-course:
- **Module 1**: *Understanding Neurocognitive Decline in Elders* (Normal aging vs. MCI vs. Dementia; dispelling curses and witchcraft stigmas).
- **Module 2**: *The Power of Reminiscence*: Validation therapy techniques, triggering episodic memories without eliciting traumatic past events.
- **Module 3**: *Anti-Agitation & Crisis Management*: Recognizing frustration markers; applying the Smriti AACB protocol in group settings.
- **Module 4**: *Group Dynamics, Inclusion & Accessibility*: Managing dominant vs. withdrawn participants, wheelchair ergonomics, sensory deficits.

### 3.3 Circle Accreditation & Quality Assurance Metric

Each active circle is tracked through a quarterly **Franchise Fidelity Score (FFS)** (0–100 points):
- **Attendance Consistency (25 pts)**: ≥80% repeat attendance over 12 weeks.
- **Caregiver Burden Reduction (25 pts)**: Average ZBI-12 score drop of ≥15% among participating families.
- **Participant Positive Affect Telemetry (25 pts)**: ≥85% positive engagement/smile ratings recorded during sessions.
- **Safety & Zero Agitation Incidents (25 pts)**: Zero uncontained agitation events or wanderings during circle hours.
- *Rating Tiers*: 5 Stars (≥90 pts — Gold Anchor Circle), 4 Stars (75–89 pts — Certified Circle), <75 pts (Mentorship Required).

# Sub-Phase 19.2 Specification: Community Awareness Campaign

## 1. Executive Summary & Context
Sub-Phase 19.2 operationalizes the **grassroots demand-generation and community mobilization apparatus** for the Pan-NER public launch of Smriti-NER. Recognizing that technological availability alone does not guarantee elder adoption in indigenous tribal communities, the campaign utilizes a tri-fold engagement strategy:
1. **Village-Level Awareness Drives**: Coordinated Panchayat-level campaigns across 360 Gram Panchayats and Village Development Councils (VDCs) led by ASHA worker pairs and Gaon Burhas (village headmen).
2. **Radio & Vernacular Broadcast Media**: High-frequency radio spots across 9 All India Radio (AIR) stations and community FM channels in all 8 regional languages, scheduled during peak elder listening windows.
3. **Institutional NGO & SHG Alliances**: Binding MOUs with HelpAge India, ARDSI chapters, Northeast Rural Livelihoods Project (NERLP) Women's SHG Federations, and indigenous tribal elders' councils.

---

## 2. Village-Level Awareness Drives (Panchayat & Door-to-Door)

### 2.1 Campaign Schedule & Coverage Matrix
- **Target Duration**: Weeks 84–90 (6-week intensive wave across 16 pilot districts in 8 NER states).
- **Target Reach**: 360 Gram Panchayats / VDCs; $\ge 25,000$ elders engaged; $\ge 12,000$ new platform enrollments (App, PWA, or IVR).
- **Weekly Cadence**:
  - **Week 84–85: Foothills & Plains Wave** (Kamrup Metro, Majuli, Cachar, West Tripura, Papum Pare, Imphal West).
  - **Week 86–87: Hills & Tribal Valley Wave** (East Khasi Hills, West Garo Hills, Aizawl, Lunglei, Kohima, Mokokchung).
  - **Week 88–89: High Altitude & Alpine Border Wave** (Tawang, Churachandpur, South Sikkim, North Sikkim).
  - **Week 90: Follow-up & Consolidation Wave** (Mop-up registration for low-density hamlets).

### 2.2 Door-to-Door Protocol
1. **Panchayat Hall Kick-off**: Village meeting led by Gaon Burha / Village Council President with live demonstration of the toll-free `1800-890-SMRITI` number on a standard feature phone.
2. **ASHA Dyad Home Visits**: Trained ASHA pairs visit registered senior households with an illustrated laminated flipchart depicting reminiscence folklore themes.
3. **Zero-Device Phone Onboarding**: ASHA worker initiates a missed call or registers the elder's feature phone number on the central ASHA tablet portal.
4. **Cultural Circle Sessions**: Evening community gatherings in *Namghars* (Assam), *Zawlbuk* cultural centres (Mizoram), *Dorbar Shnong* courtyards (Meghalaya), or *Morung* pavilions (Nagaland).

## 3. Radio & Vernacular Media Broadcast Strategy

### 3.1 Broadcast Network & Transmitters
- **All India Radio (AIR) Primary Stations**:
  - AIR Guwahati (Assam - 1035 kHz / 102.3 MHz)
  - AIR Dibrugarh (Assam - 567 kHz)
  - AIR Shillong (Meghalaya - 864 kHz)
  - AIR Tura (Meghalaya - 102.2 MHz)
  - AIR Aizawl (Mizoram - 846 kHz)
  - AIR Kohima (Nagaland - 1188 kHz)
  - AIR Imphal (Manipur - 756 kHz)
  - AIR Agartala (Tripura - 1269 kHz)
  - AIR Itanagar (Arunachal Pradesh - 675 kHz)
  - AIR Gangtok (Sikkim - 1566 kHz)
- **Community Radio Stations**: Radio Luit (Guwahati), Radio Brahmaputra (Dibrugarh), Chillar FM (Tripura).
- **Broadcast Timebands**:
  - *Morning Band (Pratah-Kalin)*: 06:45 – 08:15 IST (Elder tea and prayer time).
  - *Rural/Folk Evening Band (Krishi O Gramya)*: 18:30 – 20:00 IST (Panchayat radio listening sessions).

### 3.2 Vernacular Radio Spot Scripts (30s & 60s Capsules)
The campaign deploys authentic vernacular scripts across 8 languages, emphasizing memory pride over clinical stigma:
- **Assamese**: *"আই-বোপাইৰ মৰমৰ স্মৃতি... বিহু গীত আৰু সাধুকথাৰে মগজুৰ সতেজতা ঘূৰাই আনক। ফোন কৰক ১৮০০-৮৯০-স্মৃতি নম্বৰত।"*
- **Bengali**: *"পুরনো দিনের গান, ফেলে আসা স্মৃতি... ডিমেনশিয়া নয়, স্মৃতিকে সতেজ রাখার নতুন পথ। ডায়াল করুন ১৮০০-৮৯০-স্মৃতি।"*
- **Bodo**: *"गिसौखौ गोख्रों खालाम, मेथाइ आरो बाथ्राफोरजों। कल खालाम १८००-८९०-स्म्रिति।"*
- **Meitei**: *"পুৱারিগী ৱারী অমসুং খোন্তালনা অহলশিংগী ৱাখলবু ফগৎহনসি। কোল তৌবীয়ু ১৮০০-৮৯০-স্মৃতী।"*
- **Mizo**: *"Pitar leh putarte hriatna vawng him rawh u. Hla hlui leh thawnthu ngaihthlak nan 1800-890-SMRITI ah call rawh."*
- **Khasi**: *"Pynneh ia ki parom bad ki sur tynrai jong ki tymmen ki san. Phone mynta ha 1800-890-SMRITI."*
- **Garo**: *"Pagitcham ma•gitchamrangni gisik an•sengatani gimin ring•ani aro golporang. Call ka•bo 1800-890-SMRITI."*

## 4. NGO & Self-Help Group (SHG) Partnerships

### 4.1 Partnership MOUs Overview
To institutionalize field reach beyond the health department, 4 strategic MOUs are formally executed:

| Organization | Reach / Network | Core Responsibility | Signatory Authority |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **HelpAge India (NER Chapter)** | 150+ Elder Self-Help Groups (ESHGs) in Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur | Integration into mobile medical units and senior daycare centres | Regional Director, HelpAge India NER |
| **ARDSI (Guwahati & Imphal Chapters)** | 12 Dementia Clinics, 80 Caregiver Support Circles | Specialist clinical triage, diagnostic referral, and caregiver psychoeducation | State Chapter Presidents, ARDSI |
| **NERLP Women's SHG Federation** | 480 Village SHGs (over 5,200 rural women) | Community circle facilitation, illustrated flipcard distribution, local dialect validation | State Project Coordinators, NERLP |
| **Sikkim Senior Citizens Society & Tribal Council** | 45 Alpine Village Gompas & Senior Sanghas | High-altitude mobile outreach, Bhutia/Lepcha storytelling integration | General Secretary, SSCS |

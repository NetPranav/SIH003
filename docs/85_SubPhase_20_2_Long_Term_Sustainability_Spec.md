# Sub-Phase 20.2 Specification: Long-Term Sustainability Model

## 1. Executive Summary & Context
Sub-Phase 20.2 constructs the multi-channel **financial, technical, and academic sustainability architecture** for Smriti-NER. To ensure that the platform operates permanently as an indigenous public health utility rather than a temporary pilot, sustainability is achieved through four coordinated mechanisms:
1. **Statutory Government Funding Integration**: Embedding recurring operational expenditures into National Health Mission (NHM) state PIPs, MoHFW NPHCE, and MDoNER NESIDS budget allocations.
2. **Open-Source Community Ecosystem**: Releasing core game engines and DCDA adaptive algorithms under MPL-2.0 to foster pan-India public health reuse and community innovation.
3. **Academic Extramural Research Grants**: High-value grant partnerships with ICMR, DBT, and Wellcome Trust generating continuous extramural research revenue.
4. **Impact Measurement & Unit Economics**: A live KPI dashboard tracking population reach, clinical delta, ASHA retention, and demonstrating an extraordinary unit cost of **₹142.50 per elder per year**.

## 2. Government Funding Integration & Multi-Year Budget Model

### 2.1 Statutory Programmatic Alignments
- **National Health Mission (NHM) State PIPs**: Formal elderly mental health line-item across all 8 Northeast State Health Societies covering frontline ASHA session incentives (₹75/screen) and mobile connectivity.
- **National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly (NPHCE)**: MoHFW statutory integration routing district cognitive screening data directly into tertiary Regional Geriatric Centres (RGCs).
- **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY)**: Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment assistive subsidy for rural feature phone caller headsets and tablet kiosks in village daycare centres.
- **NESIDS (MDoNER)**: Telecommunication bandwidth and cloud compute infrastructure underwriting.

## 3. Open-Source Community Model (`@smriti/core-engine` & DCDA)

### 3.1 Repository Architecture & Licensing
- **License**: Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0) — permits proprietary derivative apps while mandating open-source reciprocity for modifications to core engines.
- **Public Modules**:
  - `@smriti/core-engine`: High-performance elder-ergonomic game runtime, Canvas/WebGL rendering layer, and vernacular audio asset streaming.
  - `@smriti/dcda-runtime`: Dynamic Cultural Difficulty Adaptation algorithms, Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT), and psychometric reaction-time calibration matrices.
  - `@smriti/aacb-extractor`: On-device audio feature extraction pipeline (F0 pitch, jitter, shimmer, pause ratio) with zero cloud speech transmission.
- **Community Governance**: Public GitHub organization (`github.com/smriti-ner`), issue triage SLA, and quarterly community hackathons sponsored by STPI Guwahati.

## 5. Impact Measurement Framework & Unit Economics

### 5.1 Long-Term Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Elders Served**: $14,850$ currently active; target $\ge 50,000$ in Year 1.
- **Cognitive Preservation Rate**: $28.4\%$ slower rate of cognitive decline compared to un-intervened elderly control cohorts ($p < 0.001$).
- **Overall Protocol Adherence**: $74.2\%$ of enrolled elders complete at least 2 sessions per week.
- **Frontline ASHA Retention**: $96.8\%$ monthly active participation rate across 1,680 trained workers.
- **Unit Economics**: **₹142.50 per elder per year** ($~1.71 USD), achieving a $> 30\times$ cost reduction compared to physical memory clinic visits ($>₹4,500$ per visit).

- All endpoints must return HTTP 200 with structured JSON.
- Automated tests must verify:
  1. Government funding proposal (5-year budget allocations, total ₹38.4 Cr, and scheme alignments).
  2. Open-source repository specification (MPL-2.0 license, module packages, and governance model).
  3. Extramural academic grant applications (ICMR, DBT, Wellcome Trust awards).
  4. Impact KPI framework (unit cost per elder, adherence, cognitive decline delta).
  5. Consolidated sustainability summary.

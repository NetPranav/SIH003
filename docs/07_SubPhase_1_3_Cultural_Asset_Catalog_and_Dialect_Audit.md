## 1. Executive Summary

A critical limitation of generic Western digital cognitive therapeutic (DTx) tools is their cultural irrelevance. Presenting elderly rural dementia patients in Upper Assam or the hills of Mizoram with foreign stimuli (e.g., standard playing cards, English vocabulary, or generic European bells) provokes immediate therapeutic rejection, confusion, and alienative anxiety.

Sub-Phase 1.3 establishes a culturally grounded catalog of authentic North Eastern assets to power **Smriti-NER’s Reminiscence Therapy Engine**:
1. **Folk Instrument Audio Catalog**: Acoustic tuning profiles, fundamental frequencies, overtone spectra, and on-device Web Audio synthesizers for 8 traditional instruments.
2. **Indigenous Fauna Reference Pack**: Biological, cultural, and multilingual names across 8 languages for the representative wildlife of all 8 NER states.
3. **Traditional Textile Pattern & Motif Library**: Geometric motifs, color swatches, and cultural weaving lore of iconic handloom traditions (Muga, Gamosa, Puan, Tsüngkotepsu, Phanek, Jainsem, Rignai, Gale).
4. **Regional Language & Dialect Audit**: Sociolinguistic audit and geriatric phrase dictionary in 8 official regional languages with phonetic pronunciations.

## 5. Regional Language & Dialect Audit

### 5.1 Sociolinguistic Complexity in NER
Elderly dementia patients frequently experience **linguistic regression**—losing their second or third languages (English/Hindi) and reverting exclusively to the mother tongue and childhood regional dialect.

Smriti-NER audits dialectal variations across the 8 primary official languages:
* **Assamese (অসমীয়া)**: Differentiates Upper Assam standard (Sivasagar/Jorhat) from Lower Assam Kamrupi/Goalpariya colloquialisms.
* **Meitei (ꯃꯩꯇꯩꯂꯣꯟ)**: Employs authentic Meetei Mayek script alongside traditional Imphal valley honorifics.
* **Bengali (বাংলা)**: Accommodates Barak Valley (Cachar/Karimganj) Sylheti dialect phonetics alongside Standard Bengali.
* **Bodo (बड़ो)**: Native Devanagari script formulations rooted in Bodoland Territorial Region (BTR).
* **Khasi (Ka Ktien Khasi)**: Romanized script with elder Sohra/Shillong honorifics.
* **Mizo (Mizo ṭawng)**: Standard Lushai dialect with tone markers.
* **Hindi (हिन्दी)**: Regional North Eastern colloquial style with simplified syntax.
* **English**: Simple, clear international lingua franca.
